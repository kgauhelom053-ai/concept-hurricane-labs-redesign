Transparency

  • Device or technology that triggered the alert
  • Signature or rule that triggered it

Managed SOC FAQ

Where are your SOC analysts based?

In the United States. U.S.-based analysts cover every shift, every weekend and every holiday, with no offshore triage, no contractors and no handoffs at midnight.

What is included in a SOC ticket?

Concept notes

What I’d fix on the live site

Checked on 5 October 2026. These are things I observed on hurricanelabs.com, and this page shows how each one could look once fixed.

  • Same title on every core page. All 25 non-blog pages in the sitemap use the title “Hurricane Labs”. This page uses a unique, keyword-led title.
  • One shared meta description. The same 203-character description appears on all 25 pages, and it’s long enough that search results cut it off. This page has its own 152-character description.
  • No structured data. None of the 25 pages has JSON-LD. This page adds Organization, Service, BreadcrumbList and FAQPage.
  • Headings and alt text. The homepage has two H1s, an H5 testimonial straight after an H2, and empty alt text on every image. Here: one H1, an ordered heading structure, and descriptive alt text.
  • Link previews show the logo. Every page shares the same wide wordmark as its share image, favicon and home-screen icon. This page has its own 1200×630 share image.
  • Stats that crawlers can’t read. The homepage counters show up in the HTML as “0 1 2 3 4 5 6 7 8…”. Here the figures are plain text.
